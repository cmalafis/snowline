# Local knowledge — ground truth for Snowline

How runs actually face and ski, from someone who skis them. Snowline's data
pipeline checks its computed aspects against this file, and the scoring model
is tested against the "skis best" notes. Fill in what you know; skip what you
don't. Ten solid runs per resort beats forty guesses.

Aspect: the direction you face when you look straight down the fall line
(N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW).

General patterns (e.g. "front side is firm until 11 in spring", "trees in
Shadows hold powder for days", "Storm Peak goes on wind hold above X mph"):

- Lifts start slowing at about 35 mph sustained wind; the threshold varies by lift orientation. The base is sheltered and can be calm while the top is blowing (research: Steamboat blog, Jake Ingle Q&A, Mar 2024).
- The upper lifts are the first to go on wind hold: Storm Peak, Sunshine, Morningside, Four Points and the Gondola (Mar 30 2023), and Morningside, Sundown, Sunshine, South Peak and Storm Peak (Apr 5 2022). The gondola is sensitive to south wind mid-line and east wind at the top (research: Steamboat Pilot; Steamboat blog).
- Trees hold powder for days after a storm. Conifer stands keep good snow longest, while aspen glades let sun in and bake to crust (research: PeakRankings; coloradotopia; dcski forum 2008).
- North-facing terrain keeps colder, chalkier snow. The Mt. Werner chutes and No Names get little sun and stay soft (research: FirstTracks 2001; firsttracks forum 2006; Steamboat Chamber 2018).
- On windy or flat-light days, ski the trees: they block the gusts and give the eye contrast (research: austintravels).
- The main groomers (Heavenly Daze, Vagabond, and the busy runs near the tops of lifts) are best early. They get scraped by about 10:30 (research: firsttracks forum; Reddit r/steamboat via search; dcski 2001).
- The Sunshine pod natural-snow runs (One O'Clock, Two O'Clock, Rolex, Flintlock, Quickdraw) aren't groomed daily until the base is deep enough. Fewer runs are groomed after even small snowfalls (research: Steamboat blog Dec 2023; PeakRankings).
- Morningside Park usually has better snow and fewer people than the lower front side (research: firsttracks forum 2006).
- Mahogany Ridge and Fish Creek Canyon are uncut and ungroomed. Fish Creek involves a long traverse or hike back to Mahogany Ridge Express (research: Sotheby's Steamboat blog Feb 2024; PeakRankings).
- The mountain largely faces west, so the front side gets afternoon sun (research: firsttracks forum 2006; DEM agrees: most zones average W-NW).
