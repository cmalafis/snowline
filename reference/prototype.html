How the scores work

The page computes the sun's exact position for the resort and time, then works out how much sunshine each slope has absorbed since sunrise based on the direction it faces and its pitch. Combined with air temperature at that run's elevation, that tells it whether the surface is still frozen, softening, or turning to slush.

A refreeze is assumed when yesterday got above freezing at the run's elevation and last night dropped well below it. Then firm groomers and bumps improve as they soften, and peak before they go heavy. On a powder day, fresh snow is worth the most early and lasts longer in trees and on shady slopes. Popular groomers lose their corduroy faster. Flat light favors trees; strong wind penalizes exposed upper lifts.

About the trail data

This is a starter set of runs grouped by zone, and the slope directions are zone-level estimates, not surveyed values. If a run faces differently than shown, correct it and the scores update. Always check the resort's grooming report and open terrain. steamboat.com · breckenridge.com
